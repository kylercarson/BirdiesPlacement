A senior living resource for Lubbock professionals

For hospital discharge teams, case managers, social workers, and other professionals supporting families.

How to connect a family

With the family’s permission, share our contact information or call to discuss whether Birdie’s can help. Start with general information, the preferred location, and the timeline. Do not send clinical records through the website form.

Our role

We offer non-medical guidance, help families explore options, and coordinate tours and placement conversations. Clinical recommendations, discharge planning, and provider admission decisions remain with the responsible professionals and facilities.

Time-sensitive requests

Call directly and explain the timeline. Confirm availability with us; no response time or admission is guaranteed. Families do not pay for our guidance. Facilities pay referral fees after move-in.
