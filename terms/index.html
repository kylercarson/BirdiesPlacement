About Birdie’s

Birdie’s Senior Living Solutions offers non-medical referral and placement guidance to families in Lubbock and surrounding areas. It does not operate care communities or provide medical care.

Information and decisions

Website content provides general information and is not medical, legal, insurance, or financial advice. Discuss clinical needs with qualified clinicians and confirm services, prices, licensing, eligibility, and coverage directly with providers and relevant programs.

No guaranteed admission or availability

Providers determine admission and the services they can deliver. Birdie’s does not guarantee placement, availability, benefits, or outcomes. Final placement decisions belong to the family.

Fees and referral relationships

Birdie’s referral and placement guidance is provided at no cost to families. Facilities pay referral fees after move-in. Care providers charge separately for their services. Ask Birdie’s about referral relationships for any community you are considering.

Contact forms and external services

Use the contact form for general inquiries only. It is not an emergency service. Do not submit sensitive medical or financial records. See our Privacy Policy for information about the form and third-party services.
