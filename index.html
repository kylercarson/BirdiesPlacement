Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

A few common questions families ask us.

Does Birdie’s provide medical care?

No. Birdie’s Senior Living Solutions is a referral and placement service only. We help families explore options and connect with communities and providers.

Do families pay for your services?

No cost to families. Referral fees are paid by facilities after move-in.

Do you guarantee admission to a facility?

No. We do not guarantee facility admission. Final placement decisions belong to the family, and admissions are determined by each community or provider.

What types of care settings do you help with?

We help families navigate independent living, assisted living, memory care, skilled nursing, hospice, home health, and personal care options.

What areas do you serve?

We help families in Lubbock and surrounding areas. Call to confirm whether we can support your preferred location.

How much does senior living cost in Lubbock?

Costs depend on the setting, services, room type, and assessed support needs. Request a current written quote including fees and care charges. We can help you compare questions to ask; we do not publish unverified local price estimates.

Can you help with an upcoming hospital discharge?

Call and explain the discharge timeline. We can discuss available next steps; availability and admission are determined by providers. Keep your hospital discharge team involved. This website is not monitored for emergencies.

Can you help us explore Medicaid pathways?

We can help organize questions about payment options and providers. Eligibility, covered services, and participation must be confirmed with the relevant program and facility; Birdie’s does not determine benefits or guarantee coverage.

How do referral fees affect the options we discuss?

Facilities pay referral fees after move-in. Ask us which communities have referral relationships with Birdie’s and whether we can assist with a specific community. You make the final choice; no admission or placement is guaranteed.
