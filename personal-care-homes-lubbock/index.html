Understanding this option

Personal care homes are smaller residential settings that may offer assistance with daily routines. The term can describe different arrangements, so ask each provider about its license, services, staffing, and admission criteria rather than relying on a label alone.

When families explore this setting

Some families prefer a smaller household environment and a more intimate daily routine. The right fit depends on the individual’s needs, preferences, and the provider’s ability to deliver the required support.

How Birdie’s helps

We begin by listening to your family’s priorities, preferred location, budget, and timeline. We help you explore options, coordinate tours, and organize questions for communities and providers. The family makes the final decision, and each provider determines whether it can meet the person’s needs.

Questions to ask before deciding

Ask about caregiver coverage, overnight staffing, accessibility, medication assistance, meals, activities, licensing, and how the home handles a change in care needs. Visit the actual home before making a decision.

Take notes during tours and ask for written descriptions of services. Include your loved one in the conversation whenever possible. Explain routines, preferences, and practical needs so a provider can discuss whether its setting is appropriate.

Understanding costs

Request a current written quote from each provider. Ask about the base rate, one-time charges, care-related fees, deposits, cancellation terms, and what happens if support needs increase. Prices and availability change, so compare the same services rather than relying on a single advertised number.

If you plan to use insurance or public benefits, confirm eligibility and coverage directly with the program, insurer, and provider. Birdie’s does not determine eligibility, offer financial advice, or guarantee payment.

What does Birdie’s guidance cost?

Families do not pay for our referral and placement guidance. Referral fees are paid by facilities after move-in. Ask which options have referral arrangements with us and discuss any community you are considering.

How do we get started?

Call with your preferred location and timeline. For a hospital discharge, keep the discharge team involved and tell us when next steps are needed. Provider availability and admission cannot be guaranteed.
